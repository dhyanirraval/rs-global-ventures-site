Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how RS Global Ventures collects, uses, and protects information from website visitors, contact form submissions, WhatsApp enquiries and business communications.

Information Collected

How Information Is Used

We use this information to respond to product enquiries, assess requirements, coordinate sourcing and export support, and improve our website and customer service. We do not sell personal information.

Storage and Protection

We store information only as long as needed for business communication, service delivery and reasonable legal or operational requirements. We take practical security steps to protect information, but no system can guarantee absolute security.

Sharing of Information

Information may be shared with trusted service providers or business partners when needed to fulfil a request, arrange shipping, or provide compliance support. We do not provide personal data for unrelated marketing purposes.

Cookies and Analytics

The website may use analytics or cookies to better understand usage and improve experience. You may disable cookies in your browser settings.

User Rights

Where legally applicable, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by contacting us using the website contact details.

Data Retention

We keep information only for as long as necessary to conduct business, respond to enquiries, support operations and comply with legal requirements.

Changes
