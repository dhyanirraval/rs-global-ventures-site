Shipping & Delivery Policy

This page explains shipping options, responsibilities for duties and taxes, and delivery expectations for domestic and international orders.

Shipping Methods

We arrange shipping via trusted carriers and freight forwarders. Options and transit times depend on the destination, chosen incoterm, and carrier schedules.

Delivery Timelines

Delivery timelines are estimated and may vary. RS Global Ventures does not guarantee fixed delivery dates unless contractually agreed.

Customs, Duties & Taxes

Buyers are responsible for customs clearance, duties, and local taxes unless otherwise agreed in writing. RS Global Ventures can assist with documentation.

Delays

Delays may occur due to customs, carrier issues, weather, regulatory checks, or other factors outside our control.

Tracking

Tracking is provided when available. Please contact us with order details to request tracking information.
